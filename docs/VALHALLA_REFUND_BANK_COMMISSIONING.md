# Refund and bank evidence commissioning contract

**November 24, 2026. Status: prepared for review.** The connected local run uses a signed payment fixture and a separately signed statement fixture. Those actors make no claim about Stripe delivery, refund success, bank posting, accounting or funds availability. The following gates require separate authority and an isolated environment.

The existing accounting correction service is downstream of independently read processor evidence and has additional accounting controls. Use sandbox readback-only acceptance for this gate. Commercial posting and independently matched bank proceeds require further evidence.

## Independent read-only bank and accounting

1. **Approve scope and sources.** Owner designates the exact CAD entity, bank account, Stripe destination, allowed viewing scope, separate operator/credential custodian, privacy retention and cutoff. Approve either an institution-backed transaction feed carrying actual posting traces or a separately authenticated bank statement/export from the institution. The existing local statement fixture lacks those credentials and remains a fixture. Give the feed no transfer initiation, beneficiary creation, ACH or write-through to books. Confirm the accountant's separate review authorization.
2. **Configure a test connection first.** For the existing Plaid adapter use `VALHALLA_PLAID_SETTLEMENT_MODE=sandbox`, exact `VALHALLA_PLAID_STRIPE_DESTINATION` and `VALHALLA_PLAID_BANK_ACCOUNT_ID`, and a separate read-only Transactions access token stored as `VALHALLA_PLAID_READ_ACCESS_TOKEN` alongside server-only `PLAID_CLIENT_ID` and `PLAID_SECRET`. The existing implementation uses a bounded `/transactions/get` window. Prove completeness and that the specific institution supplies the payout trace. For new ongoing feeds, design a cursor-based `/transactions/sync` adapter with overlap-safe recovery and explicit added, modified and removed processing. A pending or later removed posting can't support reliance. [Existing Plaid implementation](../services/api/app/banking/plaid_transactions.py) · [Plaid transaction states](https://plaid.com/docs/transactions/transactions-data/)
3. **Match distinct evidence.** Read the exact payout ID, destination, settlement date, CAD net, all balance-transaction members and their gross, fees and net using Stripe restricted readback. Obtain exactly one independently posted and non-pending bank movement with same trace/direction, an allowed posting date, CAD amount and bank account. Reject incomplete windows, ambiguous trace, duplicate movement, balance mismatch, wrong account and re-use of any posting or member. Verify reversal or deletion triggers a hold and retains original history. Sandbox rows and signed local statements never prove deposit or spendable cash. [Bank booking contract](../services/api/app/services/stripe_bank_booking.py)
4. **Accounting review.** Accountant approves account-to-entity scope, CAD reporting, classification, period boundaries, supporting evidence and re-entry rules. Demonstrate balanced clearing, separate fees, liability until delivery acceptance, a supported refund/reversal and no recognition of unmatched or test evidence. Review cash holds, restrictions, reserves and available cash separately. For an external accounting service, commission a country-appropriate isolated workspace and least privileges. If the service cannot enforce read-only operations with its credential, use a restricted export or deliberately read-only intermediary until approved. No bookkeeping writes under this authorization.
5. **Close or abort.** Record independent provider and accountant receipts, matching hashes, scope/time and any missing rights or traces. Prove callback replay, cursor replay/correction, restart, pending and reversed acceptance cases, isolation between two Legacies, and cash held during readback outage. Hold on missing or contradictory evidence, preserve prior postings and revoke readers on cutoff. For any later real deposit, retain the bank's independent evidence and the qualified accounting disposition before reliance. Setup costs and access depend on the chosen institution and provider and remain unverified.
