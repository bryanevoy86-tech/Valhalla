## Mandate and unresolved decisions

Heimdall Prime is the owner's private coordinating intelligence. Its job is to discover, assess, build, acquire, deliver, operate, improve and expand lawful businesses within mandates. The Product Foundry can commercialize bounded subsystems. The Venture Foundry evaluates and operates independent ventures. All specialists work across authorized domains. Independent Loki challenges their evidence, budgets and decisions. Mimir retains verified outcomes, and Builder implements tested, governed changes. The owner reserves mandates, risk/spending limits, material exceptions and formal authority. An authorized technician, commercial representative, support desk or professional covers exceptions beyond Heimdall's authority or physical ability. If one is absent, the affected action holds and the dashboard shows why.

## Day-one operating proof and stop procedure

1. Seed one approved disposable Legacy, separate owner, buyer, denied tenant, Prime and independent reviewer, expiry, CAD price, documented legal/rights fixture, and scoped mandate. The owner authorizes that scope once and can intervene. Local evidence has no commercial effect.
2. Have Prime ingest one dated public source or provider fixture, request proactive role findings and route the exact digest for Loki. Invalidate the source, prove blocked dispatch, then re-review the revised source. Compare materially different models and record the stronger permitted candidate or abstention. Stage a rights-bound artifact.
3. Poll the authorized acquisition queue. Verify disclosed voluntary entry, suppression, actor-specific consent and one attribution receipt. Accept fixed terms using the disposable buyer's own account and create exactly one order. Enroll the buyer and deny another tenant. These acts belong to the synthetic buyer, independent reviewer and system workers.
4. Post a signed staged payment, replay it, deliver the exact artifact and verify buyer acknowledgment with separate liability/recognition. Submit a support request through a selected service lane, post/reconcile a scoped refund, and collect automatic Prime closeout. Exercise partial/exception/stopped refund and ensure a named support delegate owns exceptions.
5. Stop a worker mid-flight, recover, and read the one receipt, balances and holds. Keep external dispatch disabled. Revoke buyer and worker access, restore to a separate target without re-sending, and verify alerts and Mimir retention. Retain outcome and publish only the redacted evidence bundle and internal decision. If Mimir evidence is stale or revoked, block reuse. Capture exact SHA, actor per transition, timestamp, scope, permission version and independent evidence grade.
6. Abort on an unauthorized action, wrong-tenant result, stale or missing Loki receipt, wrong mode key, token exposure, duplicate external effect, unexpected spend, or a missing monitored operator. Stop the scope. Suspend the worker, disable callbacks, revoke trial identities and routing, preserve the audit trail, restore only to the isolated target, and prohibit replay until approved.

**What was exercised.** The connected product test covers comparative assessment, separate Loki evidence, scoped Prime execution, listing, checkout, synthetic acquisition/financial events, buyer entitlement/delivery/acknowledgment, refunds, accounting holds and learning. Chromium exercises disposable owner and buyer access plus the participant flow. PostgreSQL recovery runs separately. The added Prime route records a full, reconciled fixture refund closeout without another owner action. Product publishing decisions, synthetic campaign inputs, portions of fulfillment orchestration and material exceptions still use staged owner calls, seeded events or externally operated steps. The research round and product checkout do not yet provide one joined durable dispatch trail. **A single no-owner-routine-labor run remains blocked.** Verify an autonomous dispatch trail with routine actions made by scoped workers, actor-by-actor evidence, after-stop denial, and zero owner-operational events before asserting completion.
