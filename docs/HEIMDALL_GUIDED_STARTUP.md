# Heimdall guided startup for Valhalla Legacy Inc.

This owner-only planning flow uses the private Heimdall conversation. It records owner-reported checkpoints and dated references. A checkpoint supplies neither government confirmation nor permission to act. Candidate selection, disclosures, filings, provider activation and first-business release each require separate approval.

## Start a private session

Say **“I am ready to register Valhalla Legacy Inc. Walk me through everything and help me find the professionals I need.”** Heimdall returns the eight stages, what has been recorded in that conversation, the public filing fee estimate, the professional candidates, and the first open action. Keep the same conversation when resuming.

Use **Startup professionals** to see the candidate research or **Startup progress** for a fresh view. Retrying or reopening history displays the original dated response; a new progress turn recalculates freshness. To record a checkpoint, use `Record startup name | YYYY-MM-DD | private reference | decision`. Heimdall provides the next step key. Keep source documents in approved private storage and use a reference here. If a decision changes, use `Reopen startup name | YYYY-MM-DD | private reference | reason` and record the replacement after review. The latest record per step is shown, and older turns remain in the history. Flags recheck after 90 days or a guide version change. This interval is a planning convention. Actual professional, filing and reporting deadlines follow their applicable written terms.

## Incorporation and first-business checklist

| Stage | Prepare and record |
|---|---|
| Name and jurisdiction | Proposed **Valhalla Legacy Inc.** and alternatives. Perform a name pre-search, discuss named versus numbered and Manitoba versus federal, confirm availability, and retain reservation and registration references separately. Discuss IP and trademark clearance where relevant. |
| Structure | Compare one OpCo with a HoldCo and subsidiaries. Bring a simple ownership chart, intended activities and jurisdictions, roles, IP inventory, existing contracts and long-term acquisition, cross-border and trust questions. Obtain written counsel and tax advice on any structure beyond a straightforward launch before relying on it. |
| Professionals and budget | Verify regulatory status and conflicts, compare the same written scope, approve a shortlist, and authorize the terms of any engagement or disclosure separately. Include registry charges, legal and accounting quotes, ongoing operations and a reserve. Use Startup Comparison for quoted and estimated costs and the Independence Review for zero-additional-capital and reporting scenarios. |
| EIA before reliance | Bring the owner's dated case instructions. Record the registration notification trigger, deadline, channel, required documents, receipt and ongoing cadence. Track both business and personal income, liabilities, expenses, assets, contributions and sales of personal assets, retained profits, distributions, grants, loans and subsidies in the private monthly packet. Preserve verification and review separately from owner reports. No instruction date, deadline, eligibility or reporting exemption is inferred. |
| Incorporation | Confirm legal name, route, share classes, shareholders, registered office and directors with counsel. Prepare the required route-specific forms and any federal significant-control filing. After separately authorized filing, retain the certificate, filed articles, first resolutions, registers and minute book. Record the actual registration date and annual filing calendar. |
| Registrations and finances | Verify the CRA business number and corporation income tax account. Have the accountant confirm applicable GST/HST, payroll, Manitoba retail sales tax, permits and deadlines. Confirm business banking and signers, accounting periods, chart, retention, and exact Stripe sandbox and receiving-account scope. |
| Staging | Use the existing owner authorization package. Approve exact API and preview origins, isolated workers and PostgreSQL, reviewer and test identities, snapshot and restore target, trial window and one CAD sandbox offer. Retain explicit access denials, stop, replay, restore and refund evidence. |
| First business | Confirm rights, price, delivery, terms, support and refunds, and the exact Legacy. Review independent Loki evidence, sandbox receipts and applicable counsel/accountant sign-off. Record a separate live go/no-go. Keep cash held until an independently matched deposit and qualified accounting close. |

These estimates exclude professional fees, annual charges, taxes, and operating costs. Recheck fees and the applicable name/registration steps before authorizing a filing. ## Reproduce the first owner session

With the documented backend test configuration, run:

```bash
python -m pytest -q -s services/api/tests/test_saas_tenant_entitlements.py -k guided_startup
python -m pytest -q services/api/tests/test_startup_independence.py services/api/tests/test_commissioning_readbacks.py services/api/tests/test_loki_independent_receipt.py
node --test tests/test-weweb-owner-startup.test.mjs tests/test-weweb-owner-conversations.test.mjs
```

The guided test prints the first real response from the isolated WeWeb owner API. It records a choice, retries the same turn without a second write, resumes the plan, flags stale reporting, reopens a decision after a changed requirement, rejects a buyer and a revoked session, and walks through the final owner-recorded checkpoint without granting launch authority. The regular WeWeb conversation displays the reply using its existing escaped text renderer. The staged browser and the updated startup widget still require authorized preview acceptance.
