# Heimdall Prime — Non-negotiable Definition of Done

## Original objective

Heimdall Prime is the owner's **private**, general-purpose, cross-industry autonomous business operator. It should independently discover lawful opportunities, test evidence, assess risks and capital, obtain approval where required, build or launch ventures, attract customers, operate them, verify actual financial outcomes and safely improve its own workflows.

Prime is NOT sold as a subscription. Commercializable subordinate subsystems belong to a separate Product Foundry with tenant isolation and limited permissions. The Venture Foundry may build other business models independently, not just real estate or owner-suggested projects.

A module that has code is not automatically a finished capability. A simulated deal, returned HTTP 200, database status "sent", product created in a spreadsheet, or projected revenue does not establish real-world completion.

## Mandatory status states

1. SPECIFIED — inputs, outputs, side effects, limits, dependencies and measurable acceptance criterion exist.
2. IMPLEMENTED — real executable code exists; no fake-success branch or placeholder counts.
3. UNIT_TESTED — normal, denied, malformed, duplicate and failure cases pass.
4. INTEGRATED_TESTED — actual components interact with a disposable database and the real API or worker harness.
5. STAGING_VERIFIED — intended provider integration works with credential safety, realistic data and failure injection.
6. DEPLOYED — exact production commit, environment and active component verified on Render.
7. LIVE_OPERATION_VERIFIED — observed real action has independently verifiable proof (provider receipt, observed delivery, durable record).
8. BUSINESS_OUTCOME_VERIFIED — where applicable, paying customer delivery, revenue, fees, refunds and net actuals reconciled.

**Use DONE only when all applicable stages are satisfied.** For a technical component that has no commercial outcome, stage 7 is still required. For commerce, stage 8 is required before making revenue claims. Current stage must always be shown separately from target stage.

## Complete Heimdall Prime business lifecycle (acceptance contract)

- Discovery occurs on schedule without an owner typing opportunities into a prompt. Data source legality, consent, provenance, time, completeness and quality are recorded.
- The canonical universal evaluator ranks opportunities across relevant industries, includes dissent and failure pathways, and can abstain. Analysis is not a claim of success.
- The owner has enforceable controls for major expenses, contracts, risky outreach, sensitive information, regulated activity, banking, deployment and strategic changes.
- Work is stored durably, tied to one business instance, authorization, jurisdiction and data namespace. Restart/retry never loses a job or duplicates a live side effect.
- An actual worker performs each authorized task using real provider adapters and safe bounded execution. A missing provider must BLOCK, not return success.
- Every claimed completion has a durable receipt or independent verification, including the actual external provider outcome when relevant.
- Customer orders, delivery, returns, fees, bank settlements and operating costs reconcile into accurate financial records. The user's profit reinvestment and Fun Fund policy is enforced subject to lawful/accountant-reviewed configuration.
- Operations recover from worker death, database errors, timeouts, stale approvals, provider outage, replay, fraud and duplicate webhooks; actions quarantine when safe recovery is impossible.
- Mimir learns from real observed outcomes, with versioned and cited source evidence, without allowing synthetic history to masquerade as real achievement.
- Marketing and customer acquisition comply with platform rules and Canadian messaging rules, with permissions and auditable external side effects.
- Prime remains privately controlled; clones and commercial subsystems are separate and cannot bypass Prime owner governance.

## Small first implementation (PR #4)

The authenticated Business Factory pilot queues an internal digital-product listing draft into existing legacy work records. An explicit owner approval event is needed. A one-task runner writes a database MediaContent record. An independently checkable SHA-256 receipt is stored in LegacyOrchestrationEvent. Duplicate execution does not create a second media record in the integrated SQLite test.

**Meaning of its status COMPLETED:** the INTERNAL database listing draft was written and checksum verified. Nothing else.

**Not completed:** real downloadable product verified, external storefront listing, product purchase, payment verification, product fulfillment, marketing publication, customer acquisition, financial settlement, multi-day autonomous execution and cross-industry proactive discovery. These must be completed using the same governed task lifecycle, not separate unrelated completion trackers.

## Engineering and operational release rules

- No new packs or clones to conceal missing end-to-end connections.
- No unrestricted external side effects or self-modifying production code.
- Feature flags and secrets must default to safe/blocked.
- No uncontrolled spending, AI API consumption, customer outreach, banking actions, or paid infrastructure creation.
- Never claim a green focused test is equivalent to a green full test suite.
- No unreviewed direct commit to main; main auto-deploys to Render.
- Keep the branch, PR, tests, SHA, Render deployment verification and live evidence updated in ops/CHANGE_LEDGER.md.


## Full-vision completeness is broader than the business factory

This definition of done applies to every workstream in ops/VALHALLA_FULL_VISION_TRACEABILITY.md and all 47 entries in ops/prime_system_integration_matrix.json. In particular, neither a profitable digital product nor a functioning core engine closes the AI Gods/Pantheon, Norns/Mimir, five legacy businesses, clone/mirror, whole-life/infinite banking, trusts, Canada exit/residency, family succession, owner Fun Fund accounting, Shield/Black Ice or resort/expansion workstreams.

Insurance/tax/trust/residency systems have a different legitimate finish line from the commerce engine: a verified owner-private decision support tool, real data and professional sign-off workflow may be COMPLETE AS AN ADVISORY CAPABILITY, but a real trust formation, insurance purchase, money transfer, residency departure or tax filing can only be marked LIVE VERIFIED after actual lawful authorized proof. Do not convert long-range ambitions into automatic legal instructions. See master traceability register for conflicts requiring owner ratification.
